Golf Camera
Privacy Policy

Originally published: November 5, 2026
Last updated: November 6, 2026

onsight operates Golf Camera (also known as ゴルカメ, the “App”). This policy explains how information is handled when you use the App to record golf swings, detect poses, save and replay videos, and view videos on a connected device.

1. Information we process and why

  • Camera images and videos: Used for camera previews, swing detection, recording, replay, video comparison, and saving. Videos you choose to import are also used for in-app storage and playback. The App does not record audio through the microphone.
  • Pose information: Body joint positions are estimated from images on your device to detect the address and finish positions and display a skeleton overlay. This information is not used to identify or authenticate individuals or for medical diagnosis.
  • Settings and video management information: Camera angle, handedness, camera selection, saving and playback preferences, favorites, video identifiers, and save timestamps are used to remember your preferences and organize your video library.
  • Connection information: When you connect two devices, local network IP addresses, port numbers, device model names, and temporary connection identifiers are used to discover devices, establish a connection, and communicate.

2. Storage and transmission

Recorded and saved videos are stored in the App's private storage on your device. Pose estimation runs on your device using a model bundled with the App. The App does not automatically upload videos or pose information to servers operated by onsight.

When you start a two-device connection, detected and saved swing videos are automatically sent to the connected display device and saved there as well. The devices also exchange playback speed, video identifiers, transfer integrity information, and playback and detection control messages. Automatic discovery advertises connection information on the local network. Connection QR codes also contain connection information.

In connected mode, the camera device continues processing camera images to detect the next swing while the display device is playing a video, even when the camera preview is hidden.

3. Security and device-to-device communication

The App uses Android's private app storage and camera permission controls. However, the current device-to-device video transfer does not implement application-level transport encryption such as TLS. Use trusted devices and a Wi-Fi network you control, and do not share connection QR codes with others. The confidentiality of this communication is not guaranteed.

4. Libraries and device services

  • The App uses MediaPipe for pose detection and ZXing for QR code processing. These functions run on the device.
  • Spoken notifications use Android's text-to-speech service. The App passes predefined detection announcements to the speech engine. Depending on the selected engine and device settings, the speech service may use network communication.
  • App settings and favorites may be included in Android backup or device migration, depending on device settings. Saved videos are stored in a location excluded from Android backup.

The App does not include a separate usage analytics SDK or a custom crash-report upload feature. The advertising SDK processes usage and diagnostic information as described below. No account registration is required. It does not include features designed to access your contacts or precise location. Information independently processed by Google Play, Android, device manufacturers, or text-to-speech providers is governed by their respective privacy policies.

5. Advertising

The advertising-enabled version of the App uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display a banner on the home screen and an interstitial before entering the camera flow. Development builds use Google's test ads.

The advertising SDK collects and shares IP addresses (which may be used to estimate general location), app and ad interaction information, diagnostic and performance information, and device or account identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID. Google uses this information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. The App does not provide recorded swing videos or pose coordinates to the advertising SDK.

Advertising data is transmitted to Google using encrypted connections. This is separate from the local device-to-device video transfer described in section 3. Google's processing, retention, and applicable international transfers are described in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

The App uses Google's User Messaging Platform to obtain and manage privacy choices where required. Advertising requests are made only when that platform indicates they are permitted. When required, an “Ad privacy options” control on the home screen allows you to revisit your choices. You can also manage or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. Available ads and personalization depend on your choices, region, and service settings. Deleting local App data does not automatically delete information already processed by Google; see Google's privacy controls for available options.

6. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell your recorded videos or pose information. Advertising-related information is shared with Google as described in section 5. Apart from device-to-device transfers you initiate and the device services described above, the App does not provide a mechanism for onsight to transmit your videos or pose information to external parties.

7. Retention and deletion

Saved videos remain on your device until you delete them. Open the video library using the folder icon and use the delete control to remove a video. Clearing the App's storage or uninstalling it deletes videos and settings from that device's private app storage. Temporary replay data is kept in cache or other temporary storage and is deleted during cleanup or may be removed by Android's cache management.

A video sent to another device is an independent copy. Deleting it on the camera device does not delete it on the display device; delete copies separately on each device. Deleting an imported video from the App does not delete its original file. Manage any settings retained in an operating-system backup through your backup service.

8. Your choices and requests

You can revoke camera permission in Android settings. Doing so prevents features such as recording and QR code scanning from working. Exit connected mode to stop device-to-device transfers. You may contact us using the details below about access, correction, deletion, restriction, or other rights available under applicable law. onsight cannot access videos stored locally on your device, so you will need to manage that local data on the device.

9. Support inquiries

If you contact us, we use the email address, message, and optional attachments you provide to respond and resolve your inquiry. Do not send unnecessary videos or other people's personal information. We retain correspondence for as long as needed to handle the inquiry and delete it when no longer needed, unless applicable law requires longer retention.

11. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy and its last updated date when the App's features or data practices change. Where appropriate, we will communicate significant changes through the App or its listing pages.
