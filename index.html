Golf Camera
Privacy Policy

Originally published: November 5, 2026
Last updated: November 22, 2026

onsight operates Golf Camera (also known as ゴルカメ, the “App”) for Android and iOS. This policy applies to both versions; features and platform-specific differences are identified below. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the App to record golf swings, detect poses, save and replay videos, and view videos on a connected device.

1. Information we process and why

  • Camera images and videos: Used for camera previews, swing detection, recording, replay, and saving. On versions that offer video comparison or importing, videos you select are also processed for those features. The App does not record audio through the microphone.
  • Pose information: Body joint positions are estimated from images on your device to detect the address and finish positions and display a skeleton overlay. This information is not used to identify or authenticate individuals or for medical diagnosis.
  • Settings and video management information: Camera angle, handedness, camera selection, saving and playback preferences, daily swing counts, favorites where supported, video identifiers, and save timestamps are used to remember your preferences and organize your video library.
  • Connection information: When you connect two devices, local network IP addresses, port numbers, device names or model names, and temporary connection identifiers are used to discover devices, establish a connection, and communicate.

2. Storage and transmission

Recorded and saved videos are stored in the App's private storage on your device. Pose estimation runs on your device using a model bundled with the App. The App does not automatically upload videos or pose information to servers operated by onsight.

When you enable two-device connection mode, completed swing videos are automatically sent to the connected display device for replay. Received videos may be held temporarily or retained in the receiving device's video library according to automatic-save settings and save operations, including save requests from the camera device. The devices also exchange playback speed, video identifiers, transfer integrity information, and playback and detection control messages. Automatic discovery advertises connection information on the local network. Connection QR codes also contain connection information.

In connected mode, the camera device continues processing camera images to detect the next swing while the display device is playing a video, even when the camera preview is hidden.

When you choose a video-sharing action, the selected video is passed to the app, service, or destination you choose through the operating system's sharing interface. That destination may upload or retain its own copy under its privacy policy. The App does not share videos through this interface without your action.

3. Security and device-to-device communication

The App uses the private app storage and permission controls provided by Android or iOS. However, the current device-to-device video transfer does not implement application-level transport encryption such as TLS. Use trusted devices and a Wi-Fi network you control, and do not share connection QR codes with others. The confidentiality of this communication is not guaranteed.

4. Libraries and device services

  • Both versions use MediaPipe for on-device pose detection. Android uses ZXing for QR code processing; iOS uses Apple frameworks for QR code scanning and generation. These functions run on the device.
  • Spoken notifications use Android's text-to-speech service or Apple's speech synthesis framework on iOS. The App passes predefined announcements, not camera images or videos, to the speech service. On Android, network use may depend on the selected speech engine and its settings.
  • On Android, settings and favorites may be included in backup or device migration, depending on device settings. Saved videos use Android's no-backup storage. On iOS, saved videos are stored in the App's Documents directory and are not explicitly excluded from backup. Videos and settings may therefore be included in iCloud or computer backups or device migration, depending on your Apple and device settings. Such backups are separate from uploads by onsight.

The App does not include a separate usage analytics SDK or a custom crash-report upload feature. The advertising SDK processes usage and diagnostic information as described below. No account registration is required. It does not include features designed to access your contacts or precise location. Information independently processed by Google Play, Android, Apple (including iOS, iCloud, and the App Store), device manufacturers, or text-to-speech providers is governed by their respective privacy policies.

5. Advertising

The advertising-enabled version of the App uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display banners on the home and replay screens and an interstitial before entering the camera flow. Development builds use Google's test ads.

Depending on platform, permissions, consent choices, and SDK configuration, the advertising SDK may collect and share IP addresses and approximate location derived from them, app and ad interactions, ad impression information, crash and performance diagnostics, and device or account identifiers. Android identifiers can include the advertising ID and app set ID. On iOS, identifiers may be scoped to an app or developer, and access to the advertising identifier (IDFA) is subject to Apple's permission controls. The current iOS version does not request App Tracking Transparency authorization. Google uses this information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. The App does not provide recorded swing videos or pose coordinates to the advertising SDK.

Advertising data is transmitted to Google using encrypted connections. This is separate from the local device-to-device video transfer described in section 3. Google's processing, retention, and applicable international transfers are described in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

The App uses Google's User Messaging Platform to obtain and manage privacy choices where required. Advertising requests are made only when that platform indicates they are permitted. When required, an “Ad privacy options” control on the home screen allows you to revisit your choices. On Android, you can manage or delete your advertising ID in system settings. On iOS, you can manage app tracking permissions in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking; this system control is separate from the App's ad privacy choices. Available ads and personalization depend on your choices, region, and service settings. Deleting local App data does not automatically delete information already processed by Google; see Google's privacy controls for available options.

On Android versions offering a premium subscription, purchases and subscription management use Google Play Billing. The App receives purchase and entitlement information, including purchase tokens and subscription status, to enable premium features and acknowledge purchases with Google Play. Payment details are handled by Google Play; the App does not collect payment card details. On iOS versions offering Premium, purchases, restoration, and subscription management use Apple StoreKit and the App Store. The App checks Apple-verified transaction and entitlement information on the device to enable Premium. Apple handles payment details; the App does not collect payment card details. Google Play and App Store subscriptions are managed separately and do not transfer between stores.

6. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell your recorded videos or pose information. Advertising-related information is shared with Google as described in section 5. Apart from the connected-device transfers, sharing actions, and device services described above, the App does not provide a mechanism for onsight to transmit your videos or pose information to external parties.

7. Retention and deletion

Saved videos remain on your device until you delete them. Open the video library using the folder icon and use the delete control to remove a video. On Android, clearing the App's storage or uninstalling it removes its private local data. On iOS, deleting the App removes its local app data, while offloading the App can retain documents and data. Temporary replay data is kept in cache or other temporary storage and is deleted during cleanup or may be removed by the operating system. These operations do not delete copies in backups, on other devices, or in destinations you shared with.

A video sent to another device is an independent copy. Deleting it on the camera device does not delete it on the display device; delete copies separately on each device. Deleting an imported video from the App does not delete its original file. Manage videos or settings retained in operating-system backups through your backup service, such as iCloud or your computer backup tools.

8. Your choices and requests

You can revoke camera permission in Android or iOS settings. Doing so prevents features such as recording and QR code scanning from working. On iOS, you can also manage the App's Local Network permission in system settings. Exit connected mode to stop device-to-device transfers. You may contact us using the details below about access, correction, deletion, restriction, or other rights available under applicable law. onsight cannot access videos stored locally on your device, so you will need to manage that local data on the device.

9. Support inquiries

If you contact us, we use the email address, message, and optional attachments you provide to respond and resolve your inquiry. Do not send unnecessary videos or other people's personal information. We retain correspondence for as long as needed to handle the inquiry and delete it when no longer needed, unless applicable law requires longer retention.
